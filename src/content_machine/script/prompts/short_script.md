## What to write
1. `hook` — one sentence spoken in the first 2 seconds that makes scrolling away feel impossible.
2. `scenes` — 4 to 7 scenes. Each has:
   - `narration`: what the voice says (short, punchy, spoken-language sentences),
   - `on_screen_text`: 2–6 words shown big on screen,
   - `visual_query`: a concrete stock-footage search query (English, 2–5 words, things a camera could film; no text, logos or real people's names).
3. `cta` — the closing line.
4. `title` — ≤ 70 characters, curiosity-driven, no clickbait lies.
5. `description` — 1–2 sentences for the post caption.
6. `hashtags` — 5–10 relevant hashtags without the `#`.

Facts must be accurate and widely accepted. If something is uncertain, phrase it as such or leave it out.
